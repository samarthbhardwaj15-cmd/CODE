## 🎵 Ma Azad Ho

### Intro

Ma azad hoon aur qaid bhi,
Ma bana sanjide sa awara hoon,
Maa jo bhi hoon, tujh sa hoon,
Sahi hoon ya galat bhi...

### Verse 1

Tera lahrata falak sa jo baal hai,
Unhe dekh kar aaya patang udaane ka khayal hai,
Lada pache aur kata patang bhi,
Par tere ishq
Ek alag hi mishaal ha.

### Pre-Chorus

Tere aakneih jaisa talab hai,
Is registan bhari zindagi mein,
khud ka chehra dekh kar hi,
 Bujhti  pyaas hai...

### Chorus

To meri bate shun, mera ek sawaal ha,
mera andar ya ishq ka zehar?
Ya ulfat ka kahar?
ya tari konsi nayi chal ha
to zilat kara apman bhi
kyo mujha syikhar ha


### Outro

Yaa gulaab sa jo tere gaal hai,
Us pe til mein chhota sa bawal hai,
Teri tarashi nazrein humari taraf hi kyun?
Kya hamesha takraar hai?

Aajkal humse bata, khushboo khushboo,
Baatein bhi teri man mein kya khayal hai...

**Ma azad hoon...
Aur qaid bhi.**
